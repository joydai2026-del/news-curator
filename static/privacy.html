Privacy

This site is a static, personally curated news page. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics, and collects nothing about its readers. Preview images are loaded directly from the publishers that declared them, with no referrer information sent.

The newsletter section is produced by software that reads one dedicated, owner-operated mailbox subscribed to public newsletters, using read-only access. Story titles, links, and the newsletters' own summaries are extracted; links are stripped of tracking identifiers before they appear here, and no mailbox address, message content, or subscriber identifier is ever published or stored.

Questions: see the repository linked in the footer of the main page.
